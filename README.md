# 🚀 SRIJAN 2026

### Build. Create. Impact.

**SRIJAN 2026** is an Inter-College Hackathon organized by **CSI_SRMCEM × D’CODERS** in collaboration with **GFG SRMCEM**.

The event brings together developers, innovators, designers, and problem-solvers to build creative and practical solutions for real-world problems.

## 🏆 Hackathon Structure

### Round 1 — Screening & Shortlisting
📅 **13 October 2026**

Teams will be evaluated based on their proposed solution, innovation, feasibility, and technical approach.

Selected teams will move forward to the final round.

### Round 2 — Final Hackathon
📅 **14 October 2026**

Shortlisted teams will present and demonstrate their solutions at **SRMCEM, Lucknow**.

Projects will be evaluated by judges and industry experts.

## 🎯 Our Objective

SRIJAN aims to provide students with a platform where they can:

- Solve real-world problems
- Build innovative solutions
- Improve technical and problem-solving skills
- Experience teamwork and product development
- Interact with mentors and industry professionals
- Showcase their ideas and technical capabilities
- Explore internship and career opportunities
